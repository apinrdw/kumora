Sweep alert sounds

Throwaway prototype for the Running Trades alert. Each pair is synthesised in the browser with WebAudio, so it ships no audio files. Burst plays 4 s of an IDX open: every 250 ms update plays at most one buy and one sell, sometimes both at once, as decided on the map. Listen for whether you can tell buy from sell with your eyes closed, and whether you could stand it all session.

Press any button to start audio.
