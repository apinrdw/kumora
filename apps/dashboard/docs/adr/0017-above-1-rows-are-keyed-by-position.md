# Above-1% rows are keyed by position

Above-1% holdings rows are keyed by the row's position among its stock's rows, not by the investor's name. Neither name nor holder key is unique within a stock: (stock, exact name) repeats once in Apr 2026, and (stock, holder key) repeats 2 to 29 times a month.
