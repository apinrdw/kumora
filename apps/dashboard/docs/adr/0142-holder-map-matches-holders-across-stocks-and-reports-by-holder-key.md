# The Holder map matches holders across stocks and reports by holder key

The stock page and Holder moves follow a holder within one stock, through each report on its own: a ≥1% holder month to month, an above-5% holder day to day, with renames and returns matched by ADR 0129's passes, and never an above-5% holder to a ≥1% holder. The Holder map (apinrdw/kumora#733) draws who holds what around a stock, a holder or a custodian, so it needs one holder across stocks and across the two reports: an above-5% holder of the as-of day and a ≥1% holder of the month before it are drawn once, in the 5% band, and a holder's other stocks are found by its name.

On the Holder map a holder is its holder key (`server/holdings/holder-key.ts`), the key both parsers already store and the above-1% table already indexes (`above_1_holding_by_holder`). Within one stock's day, an investor's repeat blocks are first collapsed as Daily holders collapses them (ADR 0131), and the blocks left that share a key are one holder, their % and shares summed, as a month's rows sharing a key are one ≥1% holder. A repeat block whose name has another key folds in all the same, so that key holds nothing as itself in the stock that day: the search lists a holder by the keys the map links (`stockLinks`), never such a key, and a holder focus on a key the day lists only as such repeats is read as, and opens under, the key it folds into, the largest holding's where several (apinrdw/kumora#741's eighth review: searching `ALPHA LTD`, a repeat of `ALPHA LIMITED`, opened an empty map). Every row the read takes per block or per identity (a day's blocks, the day before's blocks of a key of several, a month's rows, Holder moves' leavings, a custodian's earlier blocks) becomes a holder through one step, `holdersByKey` in `server/holdings/holder-map-holders.ts`, keyed by stock, report, period and key: so the bands, moves, crossings, hops, Off lists, custodian links and books all see a key's blocks summed, and blocks of one key leaving together leave at their summed last stake. A crossing down out of 5% needs only its key's newest leaving, no stake, so its query groups the identities first listed under a month holder's key by that stored key and returns one row a key, within its row budget (ADR 0133). An Off list cut by its row budget (ADR 0133) drops whole groups of a holder's rows, a period's leavings or a custodian's stock-day, so it never sums a holder from part of them. The 1%-to-5% band is the month's ≥1% holders whose key no block of the stock that day carries, so a holder in both reports shows once, in the 5% band, and one at 5% or more in the month with no block that day stays in the 1% band. A 5%-band holder takes its KSEI investor type from its ≥1% rows of the month, since the above-5% report publishes none. Across stocks the map matches by the same key (apinrdw/kumora#738): a stock focus's holder links to every other stock whose day or month lists its key, a holder focus (`?holder=<key>`) is every stock that lists its key, and a link between a holder and a stock is that stock's band holder of the key, so a holder above 5% of one stock and a ≥1% holder of another shows in the 5% band of the first and the 1% band of the second. A custodian focus (`?custodian=<id>`, apinrdw/kumora#739) is every above-5% holding with a securities account at the custodian, market-wide, each holder one node by its key however many stocks it holds there. Holder moves stores its lines by ADR 0129's identity, `<key>#<n>` with the key the holder was first listed under, and no key column: the map matches a line to a holder by its name's key, and finds a holder focus's lines by the identities first listed under its key, so a holder renamed since its first listing misses its Off list there.

A link's move departs from the spec's "don't recompute" (apinrdw/kumora#733, "Moves"), the owner's call on 27 Nov 2026. A 5%-band key with one block on the day and at most one on the day before is one identity, and takes its stored move (ADR 0134). A key with several blocks on either day moves as one holder, by its blocks and securities accounts summed on both days, read from the day's and the day before's: New with none the day before (no move on the stock's first listed day, which Holder moves compares with nothing), else Up or Down by its summed shares, its % change every block's on both days, else a Transfer when shares crossed custodians between its accounts, whichever of its blocks they sit in, a custodian one by its broker code, else its name (ADR 0132). Summing its blocks' stored lines instead had a hole in three review rounds of apinrdw/kumora#741 (the second, the third and the seventh): a block arriving or leaving beside an unchanged one, two blocks' Up and Down that were a Transfer, and a block unchanged in shares with no line whose % fell as listed shares rose. A 1%-band key always takes its stored move: Major holders follows a month's holder by its key (ADR 0129), so its rows are one identity, and the above-1% report has no accounts, so no Transfer. The cost: such a key's move on the map can differ from its blocks' lines on Holder moves, and the read takes the day before's blocks and accounts of such keys, at most a day's rows (ADR 0133).

A row of a stored code that isn't a Stock code, or of a name that keys blank (legal-form words only), is no holder: the read leaves it out rather than fail every other row of the read (`server/holdings/holder-map-schema.ts`, apinrdw/kumora#741's ninth review).

The stock page and Holder moves keep their own rule: ADR 0129's identity within a stock and the glossary's "never matched to a ≥1% holder". The cost is false merges: two unrelated investors of one name, such as CPGT's two blocks of ASIA ARGENTUM ASSETS HONG KONG LIMITED, show as one holder, and a holder renamed beyond what the key ignores shows as two. The owner accepted both in the spec's grilling. Undoing the summed move means storing a key's move at parse time beside its identities'. Undoing the rest means storing an investor identity across reports, which neither IDX report publishes (the above-5% report's SID is not stored), and drawing a holder per stock and report, which leaves the map with no edges between stocks.
