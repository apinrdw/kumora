# Web

The public site: the landing page, and the waitlist visitors join from it.

## Language

**Waitlist**:
The visitors who left an email on the landing page to hear when KUMORA opens, one entry per email. An email counts once, whatever its case or surrounding spaces; joining again changes nothing. Nothing is sent to the email yet.
_Avoid_: Signup list, mailing list

**Queue number**:
A waitlist entry's place in join order, from 1, given when the email first joins and never given to anyone else. The landing page shows it as a ticket, `A-` and the number padded to four digits (`A-0042`).
_Avoid_: Position, rank, ticket number
